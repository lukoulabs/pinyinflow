PinyinFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

PinyinFlow is a keyboard for Android that converts toneless pinyin (for example ni hao) into pinyin with tone marks (nǐ hǎo). This policy explains what happens to the text you type while using it. In short: nothing you type leaves your device.

Who we are

PinyinFlow is developed by LukouLabs. Contact: lukoulabs@gmail.com.

What we collect

We collect no personal data. PinyinFlow:

  • has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the network;
  • contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking libraries;
  • requires no account and asks you for no permissions (no contacts, location, microphone, storage or similar).

How your typing is processed

All suggestions are computed on your device by a built-in dictionary. Your keystrokes are used only to show suggestions and to insert the text you choose, and they are not stored.

Data stored on your device

To improve suggestions, PinyinFlow can keep two small kinds of data in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Learned preferences: which suggestions you picked and how often, so they rank higher next time. They are recorded as pinyin words with their tones (for example kā fēi), not as sentences or messages. You can turn learning off or erase this data at any time in the app's settings (Learn from my choices / Clear learned data).
  • Personal words that you add yourself in the settings, such as names.
  • Your settings: theme, keyboard height, and so on.

PinyinFlow never learns from password fields, email or web-address fields, or fields that ask keyboards not to learn (for example, incognito tabs). This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

PinyinFlow is sold through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

PinyinFlow collects no data from anyone, including children.

Android keyboard warning

When you enable any third-party keyboard, Android warns that it "may be able to collect all the text you type". This standard message is shown for every keyboard. PinyinFlow does not collect your text, and without network access it could not transmit it.
